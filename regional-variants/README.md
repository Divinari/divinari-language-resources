# Regional Variants & Linguistic Diversity

This directory documents authentic regional variations, distinct Philippine and Latin American languages, and coexisting orthographies under `CC BY 4.0`.

> Distinct regional languages (such as Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano, Kinaray-a, Bikol, Maranao, and Tausug) are recognized as autonomous languages—never reduced to "dialects" of Tagalog.
